## Rule
The factual core must remain governed and traceable. Platform scripts may change tone, duration, structure and examples, but must link back to the canonical knowledge source.

## Localization
Localization is not literal translation. It may include terminology, cultural context, local climate, regulation, availability and consumer context. Local-market facts require their own evidence/status.

## Business relationship
Education → understanding → better decision → appropriate product → responsible commerce.

Advertising revenue and media growth are business objectives, but they must not distort educational truth or manufacture user need.
